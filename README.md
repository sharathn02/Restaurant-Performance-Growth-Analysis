# Zomato analysis

## Business problem:
  Management lacks a clear understanding of the operational and geographic drivers behind restaurant performance, customer engagement, and commercial potential across multiple global cities. Additionally, an unaddressed data anomaly exists where 22.5% of the portfolio (2,148 restaurants) holds misleading zero ratings, which distorts overall quality analytics. To optimize strategic decisions, the business must isolate these unrated establishments and use pricing and engagement proxies to uncover top-performing market segments. This analysis will define which operational services, pricing tiers, and regional hubs present the highest revenue potential.

## Analytical objective:
  the analytical objective for the analysis are the following  
    * **Network Performance:** Evaluate how restaurant performance varies across countries, cities, and price tiers.  
    * **Customer Engagement:** Identify the key factors that drive higher customer engagement based on votes.  
    * **Operational Effectiveness:** Assess if online delivery and table booking correlate with better performance metrics.  
    * **Commercial Potential:** Pinpoint the price segments and categories showing the strongest pricing and engagement combinations.  
    * **Rating Coverage Gap:** Map the concentration and operational traits of the 22.5% unrated restaurants.
