## Zomato Restaurant Performance & Growth Strategy

### Problem
Zomato faces a severe data coverage and platform trust gap, with 22.5% of its network operating without any customer ratings. Additionally, there is a low adoption rate of highly engaging operational features like table bookings, alongside sharp regional performance imbalances that limit platform visibility, customer engagement, and overall commercial value.
### Dataset
A global restaurant dataset containing performance, pricing, and operational profiles for 9,551 restaurants across 141 cities and 15 countries. Key variables include online delivery availability, table booking status, price ranges, customer ratings, votes, and geographic metrics.
### Approach
Simulated an end-to-end business analytics engagement by executing a structured SQL-based workflow. Cleaned and validated the raw restaurant metrics, then leveraged advanced querying techniques (subqueries, conditional aggregation, and CASE statements) to segment restaurants across five core business areas. Evaluated correlations between operational features and metrics like user votes or pricing tiers to map performance before building a prioritized strategic growth roadmap.
### Dashboard
A Power BI visual framework designed to track restaurant performance across city clusters, contrast user engagement between operational segments, monitor price-tier distributions, and target high-risk rating gaps. (Note: Paste your dashboard screenshot or link here).
### Key Findings
  * **Rating Coverage Gap:** Identified 2,148 unrated restaurants (22.5% of the dataset), creating a major transparency issue for customers.
  * **Table-Booking Potential:** Only 12.1% of restaurants offer table bookings, even though booking-enabled venues demonstrate significantly higher customer engagement and premium positioning.
  * **Operational/Price Variations:** Distinct differences in customer votes and average costs for two were uncovered across operational profiles and four distinct price tiers.
  * **Regional Underperformance:** Isolated severe rating gaps and operational lag in specific major markets, specifically New Delhi, Noida, and Faridabad.
### Recommendations
  * **Clear the Unrated Backlog:** Prioritize target review-generation initiatives and boost visibility campaigns for high-potential unrated restaurants to restore consumer trust.
  * **Optimize Delivery Onboarding:** Drive online-delivery adoption and streamline digital menu optimization for restaurants in lower-performing price tiers.
  * **Expand Table Booking:** Scale up table-booking infrastructure among eligible premium and mid-tier restaurants to capitalize on high-engagement segments.
  * **Target Regional Quality Programs:** Launch localized restaurant improvement and merchant support programs in high-risk cities (New Delhi, Noida, Faridabad).
### Tools
SQL, MySQL, Power BI, Microsoft PowerPoint, Excel / CSV source files.
